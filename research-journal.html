What This Is

Understanding, kept in the open.

This is not company news, and it is not a blog. It is a permanent record of how Busara Labs develops trustworthy infrastructure — written as the work happens, so the reasoning behind every decision can be followed rather than taken on trust.

The Journal grows alongside Paamoja. Where the Paamoja page shows where the initiative is today, the Journal explains how it arrived there. One is progress; the other is understanding.
