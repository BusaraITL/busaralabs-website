P-01Paamoja · A Busara Labs initiative

An observed problem, becoming infrastructure.

Paamoja is the public case study of how Busara Labs is working to turn an observed problem into trustworthy infrastructure. It is in development and formal validation is pending. Nothing is for sale and no service is currently operating.

The problem

In the informal business economy the Journal records, trade is fragmented and often depends on personal assurance: stock is hard to source consistently, delivery is uncertain, and payment carries risk for whoever moves first.

P-03 · Current vision

Four elements, proposed. Not a final design.

This is Busara Labs’ current architectural thinking. We expect it to mature as evidence is collected through the journey above. These four names are a proposal only. No journal entry records them, and none of them is built, operating, or taking payment.

  • Planned

    HuruMarket

    Where buyers would discover and shop from merchants.

  • Planned

    Duka

    The proposed merchant system.

  • Planned

    Tuma

    A proposed logistics rail, so goods move from merchant to buyer, tracked and accountable.

  • Planned

    e-pay

    A proposed escrow layer, holding payment until delivery is confirmed.

Not yet operating. Paamoja does not currently process payments, hold funds, move goods or offer accounts, and there is no sign-up. Progress is recorded in the Research Journal.
